CHAMA BYLAWS - MPATANISHI AI CONSTITUTION

1. CONTRIBUTION DEADLINE
All members must submit their monthly contribution of KES 5,000 by the 5th day of every month.

2. LATE PENALTIES
Any contribution made after the 5th day but before the 15th day shall attract a fixed fine of KES 200 (Mbao).

3. EMERGENCY EXCEPTIONS
Penalties may be waived if a member provides proof of a medical emergency involving their immediate family (spouse or children). In such cases, the late fine is waived, but the contribution must still be paid by the end of the month.

4. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes regarding fines shall be moderated by the elected Treasurer and the Mpatanishi AI engine.
